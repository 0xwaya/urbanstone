# Urban Stone Collective — Instagram Post Log

Account: @urbanstonec (posted via the saved instagram.com browser login on the automation computer)
Schedule: daily at 6:00 AM and 4:00 PM ET

One entry is appended per published post. Entries are never edited or removed.

**Analytics note:** Meta Insights (reach, impressions, engagement per post) will be compared against this log later, matching posts by date/time and post URL/ID, to see which topics, materials and promos perform best.

## Tags (required on every entry)

- **Material:** one of
  - `quartzite` (e.g. Taj Mahal)
  - `quartz` (e.g. Calacatta-style white with gray/golden veins)
  - `marble` (e.g. exotic stones like Nero Marquina)
  - `granite`
  Add the stone name in parentheses, e.g. `quartz (Calacatta Andromeda)`.
- **Finish:** one of `polished`, `leathered`, or `live edge`.

**Finish rules:**
- **Live edge is granite only.** Never tag live edge on marble (marble crumbles and chips on a live edge), quartzite or quartz.
- **Leathered** is valid on granite, marble and quartzite.
- **Polished** is valid on all materials; quartz is always tagged `polished`.
- Tag the finish actually shown and described in the post. If a post's image or caption shows an invalid combination, don't publish it; fix the post first.
